The Problem B-1

Audit tests a sample.
Fraud does not happen in the sample.

5–10%
90–95% UNAUDITED
Tested by sampling Passes through unchecked
Why the gap is wider in Nigeria
i

Documents arrive as pixels, not data

Invoices, receipts and delivery notes reach finance as scans, phone photos and paper. Payments reach the bank as ledger rows. Nothing joins them automatically.

ii

Bank narrations are free text

NIBSS narration strings carry no reliable vendor identifier, so matching a debit to the invoice behind it is manual work, every time.

What survives as a result
iii

Duplicate and ghost-vendor payments

The same invoice resubmitted with a shifted date, or paid to a vendor that does not exist. Both clear because nobody checks the 90%.

iv

Amounts split below approval tiers

Three invoices at ₦2.49m each avoid the ₦2.5m approval that one ₦7.47m invoice would have required.

v

Unapplied withholding tax

WHT not deducted on professional fees becomes a FIRS liability with penalties attached, discovered years later.

ISA 530 (Audit Sampling) requires a sample to be designed and selected; it sets no fixed percentage. The 5–10% range shown is common practice, not a prescribed rate.

Innovation B-2

Global audit AI assumes a world Nigerian finance does not run in.

Every design decision in AuditaAI starts from the right-hand column.

Invoices arrive as clean, structured electronic data

Scanned paper, phone photographs, stamped delivery notes

Bank feeds are machine-readable and keyed to vendors

Free-text NIBSS narrations with no vendor identifier

The vendor master can be trusted as a control

Account details substituted on the invoice itself

Tax schedules follow US or EU rules

FIRS WHT at 5% and 10%, VAT at 7.5%, with local exemptions

A risk score is a sufficient output

An auditor must sign — so every flag needs a citation, not a score

The defensible position is not "we added AI to audit." It is that a multimodal model reads Nigerian documents as they actually arrive, while every number an auditor signs comes from deterministic code that can be re-run.

The Solution C-1

AuditaAI divides perception from arithmetic.

A language model is excellent at reading a bad scan and unreliable at adding up. So it is only ever asked to do the first.

Perception — the model reads

Gemini multimodal extraction turns scanned invoices, receipts and purchase orders into structured fields: vendor name and TIN, invoice number and date, line items and quantities, bank account and tax details.

Output is constrained to a strict schema. A malformed read fails loudly rather than corrupting a reconciliation downstream.

Arithmetic — the engine counts

A deterministic Python engine performs three-way matching across purchase order, invoice and bank debit using decimal arithmetic — totals, tolerances, tax rates, quantity reconciliation.

The model never performs a calculation. Every figure is reproducible by re-running the same code on the same evidence.

100% Coverage

Every transaction investigated, not a sample of them.

Cited Every finding

Each exception opens the source document and the rule breached, side by side.

ISA 230 Output format

A workpaper an auditor can review, adjust and sign.

How It Works C-2

Five stages, one unbroken evidence chain.

Sources enter at the left. Nothing reaches the workpaper without a citation back to the document it came from.

A-1

Ingestion

Scanned invoices, purchase orders and bank ledger exports enter as files. No ERP integration required to start.

A-2

Extraction

Multimodal model reads each document into structured fields under a strict schema.

A-3

Three-way match

Deterministic engine ties purchase order, invoice and bank debit together on amount, date and vendor.

A-4

Forensic tests

Rule engine runs duplicate, threshold-splitting, vendor-integrity and tax tests in parallel.

A-5

Synthesis

Findings are scored, ordered by exposure, and drafted into the workpaper.

Safety rail

The model is restricted to perception and reasoning. All matching, reconciliation, tax rates and thresholds run in decimal-precision Python — an architectural boundary, not a prompt instruction. The language model has no path to a number the auditor signs.

F-02 · Critical₦7,470,000

Threshold-splitting test — clustering immediately below an authorisation limit

F-03 · Critical₦12,300,000

F-04 · High₦1,180,000

F-05 · High₦3,600,000

Weekend submission with no matching purchase order

Three-way match exception — no approved order behind the payment

Total exposure · 5 of 60 transactions ₦29,400,000

Feasibility is the claim here, not ambition. These five patterns are seeded and asserted against by name. Demo-day success means all five fire with the correct citation — and false positives on the other 55 transactions stay at zero.

AuditaAI · Zecathon 6.0 · Track 02 06 / 12
Resolution D-2

A flag is not a finding until somebody resolves it.

Investigation is half the track name. This is the other half — what happens in the minutes after the engine raises an exception.

Query

Drafts the question. The exception is packaged with its evidence — the invoice page, the ledger debit, the rule breached — and sent to the initiator for response.

Hold

Recommends suspension before settlement. On critical findings the engine raises a payment hold for an authorised officer to apply, rather than reporting the loss after the money has left.

Recover

Logs the recovery. Duplicate and overpayment findings create a tracked recovery entry that stays open until the funds are returned or written off with justification.

Escalate

Routes by severity. Each finding carries an owner, a due date and an audit trail of who saw it, what they decided and when.

Every resolution writes back into the workpaper. What reaches the auditor is not a list of alerts but a record of what was found, what was decided and what evidence supported it. That closed loop is what makes the output signable.

Trust & Data Protection E-1

Built so that an auditor can sign it, and a bank can host it.

Explainability — no black box

An auditor cannot sign off on a model's opinion. Every exception opens the original invoice page beside the ledger debit, with the discrepancy highlighted and the rule named.

Findings are reproducible: the same evidence re-run produces the same result, because the arithmetic is code rather than inference.

Data protection — the default question

Account numbers, personnel names and other direct identifiers are pseudonymised before any inference call. Model endpoints are used under zero-retention terms.

The system is containerised and deployable inside the bank's own environment, aligned to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Client data need not leave the perimeter for the product to work.

Zero-math-hallucination guarantee. Language models read documents. Python counts money. The two are separated by an architectural boundary, which is why the figures in the workpaper can be defended line by line.

Target Users E-2

Who uses it, and who pays for it.

The buyer and the daily user are different people. Both have to be served or the tool is abandoned.

Primary user

Internal audit and inspection divisions

Bank inspection teams and enterprise internal audit functions. They test a sample by hand and carry the professional risk for what it missed.

Primary user

Finance controllers and CFO offices

Own the disbursement process. They need leakage found before settlement, not an annual report describing what already left the account.

Economic buyer

Corporate and commercial banking risk

Zenith's credit teams carry the downside when a corporate client's internal leakage becomes an NPL. They have budget and a direct interest.

Channel — external audit firms

Nigerian audit practices run the same manual reconciliation on every engagement. Selling to firms multiplies reach without multiplying sales effort, and firms already hold the client relationships and the obligation to test more than a sample.

Where it starts

A single-entity deployment inside one internal audit function, on historical transactions first. Results are compared against findings the team already knows before anything touches live payments — the cheapest way to earn trust in the output.

Text size is computed, not fixed. Each slide measures its own content and takes the largest size at which it still fits the 1280×720 frame — so nothing spills and nothing is left half-empty. The toolbar readout shows what it settled on: deck ×1.34 means the whole deck is running at 1.34× the base type size, and the per-slide numbers show where individual slides landed. Reopen the file after any edit and it re-fits itself.

If a red OVERFLOW chip appears on a slide, that slide's content is longer than the frame can hold even at the smallest size. The fix is to cut words on that slide, not to shrink the type — the chip sits in the corner and never prints.

First: turn off "Headers and footers". Chrome prints the date, time, page number and file path into the page margin by default. No CSS can remove them — it is a checkbox in the print dialog, not something the page controls. Open More settings and untick Headers and footers before saving.

Then set: Destination: Save as PDF · Layout: Landscape · Paper size: 13.33 × 7.5 in (choose Custom if it is not listed) · Margins: None · tick Background graphics. Each of the 12 slides becomes exactly one page.

Before submitting: open the PDF and confirm the pages are wide, not tall, and that no date, URL or file path appears in the corners or along the bottom edge. If they come out portrait, the paper size in the dialog did not take — set it manually.
